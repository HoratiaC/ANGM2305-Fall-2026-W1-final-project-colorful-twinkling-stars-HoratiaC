## Description
- Many stars of different colors and sizes will fade in and fade out scattered across the canvas as if they were twinkling in a dark sky. 
I wanted to show an artwork with the coding we have learned.

## Features
- __Black or Dark Blue Background__
  - Most likely will use a bluish background and I will using pygame with screen.fill to display it.
- __Star Shape__
  - I will use turtle and pygame to have the shapes being drawn using a loop.
- __Fade in & out__
  - To manipulate the shapes to fade in and out I will be using pygame.
- __Size__
  - To resize the shapes I will use pygame.
- __Colors__
  - I will use turtle to change the colors.
