## Description
- Many stars of different colors and sizes will fade in and fade out scattered across the canvas as if they were twinkling in a dark sky while a car will be shown passing by at the bottom of the canvas with a landscape behind it. 
I wanted to show an moving artwork with the coding we have learned.

## Features
- __Black or Dark Blue Base Background__
  - Most likely will use a bluish background and I will using pygame with screen.fill to display it.
- __Landscape Background__
 - Will use turtle to draw either hills or mountains on top of the base backgrund.
- __Crescent Moon Shape__
 - I will display a white or yellow crescent moon at the top right of the screen with turtle.
- __Star Shape__
  - I will use turtle and pygame to have the shapes being drawn using a loop.
- __Fade in & out__
  - To manipulate the star shapes to fade in and out I will be using pygame.
- __Size__
  - To resize the star shapes I will use pygame.
- __Colors__
  - I will use turtle to change the colors of the stars.
- __Car__
 - I will use pygame to cntrol a car from left to right across the screen with keys.
